# Notes, markers, and participants

## Notes

Free-text notes you add to a conversation — context you want on record
that wasn't said out loud (e.g. "patient mentioned a symptom before
recording started"). Optionally attach a timestamp. Notes are never
generated automatically; every note is something a human explicitly
typed. They're not shown or used anywhere as if they were spoken content.

## Markers

Timestamped bookmarks, either placed live while recording (the **Marker**
button) or added afterward from the conversation's sidebar. Use them to
flag moments worth returning to. Markers appear as tick marks on the audio
player's seek bar.

## Participants

People present in the conversation. You are never required to enter a
real name — a label like "Person A", "Doctor", or "Patient" is entirely
sufficient and often preferable. Participant type (Staff/Patient/Client/
Guest/Other/Unknown) is optional context, not a required clinical field.

Participants are added and managed manually. There is no automatic
speaker detection in this release — a future phase may suggest
participant assignments from voice analysis, but only with a human
reviewing and confirming the suggestion before it's treated as fact.

**Adding a registered colleague as a participant.** Besides typing a
free-text name, you can pick "Registrierte Person" ("Registered person")
in the "Teilnehmer hinzufügen" ("Add participant") panel to add anyone
already registered in your organization — useful for staff who
regularly appear in conversations (e.g. yourself, or a colleague who was
present). Picking someone pre-fills their name into the editable text
field and links the participant record to their account, so later
views (avatar, "I was there") can recognize it — but the label you see
and can still freely rewrite is always the free-text name, never the
account. A participant that's already linked to a registered user shows
an ID-card badge next to their name. This does not change the rule
above: a real name is still never required, even for a linked
participant — you can, for example, link yourself but keep the label
"Behandler".
